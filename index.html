I am a PhD candidate at the Centre de Recherche sur le Droit et l'Économie (CRED), Université Paris 2 Panthéon-Assas, co-supervised by Bruno Deffains and Romain Espinosa.

My research focuses on the understanding and aggregation of legal indicators, and on the intensity of prison sentences.
